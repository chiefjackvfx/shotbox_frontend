Blinds Direct report: three visual review and revision passes

Baseline inspected: cover, contributor page, dense shot page, short timeline.
Findings: 13 pages, sparse short timelines, small thumbnails, cover hardware
close-up, raw task lists, missing continuation headings.

Pass 1 — layout (Blinds_Direct_Pass_1.pdf, 10 pages)
Enlarged thumbnails, chose the first visible shot with task context for the
cover, simplified the cover title, used restrained serif display typography,
and replaced fixed timeline batches with complete cards flowing across pages.
Fixed singular elapsed-day wording. Inspected cover, first shot page and both
ending pages. Short timelines now share pages; no shots were omitted.

Pass 2 — content (Blinds_Direct_Pass_2.pdf, 10 pages)
Added current task status summaries and historical approval milestones.
Replaced event-type bars with the five shots with most production changes.
Cleaned timeline labels in the donut chart and distinguished current task
status from repeated approval events. Inspected chart, contributor, middle
shot and final pages. Remaining issues: small task text and no running
section context on continued pages.

Pass 3 — polish (Blinds_Direct_Pass_3.pdf, 11 pages)
Increased secondary text size, added aligned task/artist/status tables,
quoted scope summaries, and named the outstanding matchmove approval.
Added running timeline headers and PDF outline bookmarks. Allowed additional
space for dense cards instead of shrinking type. Inspected all 11 rendered
pages and the densest shot page at larger scale.

Final verification
25 distinct shot-card headings; all 3 timelines retained.
230 total activity records; 136 production events.
21 current tasks approved; 1 waiting for approval (blinds_160, hidden shot).
23 historical transitions into Approved/Done, including repeated/deleted tasks.
206 total events lack an author; production-only chart correctly shows 126.
No budget figures; elapsed days are not represented as worked hours.
PDF text bounding boxes remain within page margins; bookmarks are present.
Final main PDF is byte-identical to inspected pass 3 (871,992 bytes).

Limitations
This remains an internal frontend script prototype, without an app button.
Task descriptions are faithful to recorded titles/notes, not inferred prose.
Unattributed history and missing task descriptions cannot be reconstructed.
